## About Me

I am an Electrical Engineering student at the University of Illinois at Urbana-Champaign, pursuing a Minor in Computer Science. I grew up on a farm in south-central Illinois, where I developed a desire to become an engineer through hands-on farm work and equipment repair.

I am currently working as an Undergraduate Research Assistant in the Agricultural and Biological Engineering Department at the University of Illinois, where we are developing an Autonomous Weeding Robot for horseradish farmers. Outside of school, I am an electronics hobbyist where i do circuit repair, mostly for my dad's farm equipment, and work on personal PCB and other engineering projects in my spare time.

I am interested in embedded systems and PCB design and layout, with a focus on agriculture, as reflected in my current research role. I hope to pursue embedded systems, PCB design, or both as a career after graduation.
